Your Day-by-Day Plan

Day 1 · Arrive & unwind

Meet at the airport and spend your first night in Negombo.

Day 2 · Echoes of ancient Ceylon

Explore Polonnaruwa before continuing to the Sigiriya area.

Day 3 · Rock fortress & village life

Visit Sigiriya, then leave time for a village experience or a relaxed afternoon.

Day 4 · Cave temples to Kandy

Stop at Dambulla on the way to Kandy and visit the Temple of the Tooth.

Day 5 · Through tea country

Travel via Nuwara Eliya and a tea stop to Ella. A train segment can be requested, subject to tickets.

Day 6 · Wildlife to the coast

Leave Ella for an Udawalawe safari and continue to Galle. This is a full travel day; agree the pace with us.

Day 7 · Farewell, island life

Travel from Galle to the airport or another agreed end point.

Suggested itinerary. Timing, transport, accommodation, meals, tickets and pickup arrangements are confirmed before booking. Wildlife sightings and train tickets depend on conditions and availability.
