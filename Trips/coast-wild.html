Your Day-by-Day Plan

Day 1 · Hello, south coast

Travel from the airport or an agreed meeting point to Weligama. Settle into the coastal pace.

Day 2 · A day by the ocean

Keep the day for the beach and local exploring. Choose your own balance of rest and activity.

Day 3 · Towards the wild

Continue inland to Udawalawe. Plan a jeep safari around the park schedule, with wildlife sightings left to nature.

Day 4 · Fort walls & sea views

Travel to Galle and explore the fort streets and ramparts at your own pace.

Day 5 · A gentle goodbye

Enjoy a final coastal morning before travelling to your agreed drop-off point.
