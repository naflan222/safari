Your Day-by-Day Plan

Day 1 · Into the cultural heart

Meet in Negombo or at the airport and travel to Kandy. Settle in and visit the Temple of the Tooth.

Day 2 · A kingdom above the trees

Travel to Sigiriya for the rock fortress. Allow time for the climb and an unhurried return to Kandy.

Head towards Colombo for a city stop if your flight schedule allows, then continue to the airport.
